# Walmart: Weekly Sales Analysis & Budget/Inventory Optimization

## Project Overview
Acting as a Commercial Data Analyst for **Walmart**, I developed an executive summary and interactive dashboard analyzing 2012 weekly sales performance. The objective was to deliver actionable business intelligence to Commercial Leadership to guide strategic budget adjustments and inventory allocation across stores and seasons.

## Key Methodology & Analytical Framework
* **Data Cleaning & Preparation:** Audited, standardized, and structured raw weekly sales datasets to ensure metric accuracy and data integrity.
* **Commercial KPI Development:** Defined and computed core retail performance indicators (weekly revenue trends, seasonal peaks, store-level variance, and holiday impact) to evaluate operational efficiency.
* **Interactive Dashboarding:** Built dynamic visual dashboards enabling leadership to filter performance drivers and spot inventory bottlenecks at a glance.
* **Executive Communication (C-F-I Method):** Structured all analytical insights using the **Context – Finding – Implication (C-F-I)** framework to translate complex sales data into clear, actionable business recommendations.

## Tools & Skills
* **Google Sheets / BI:** Data Wrangling, Retail KPI Modeling, Interactive Dashboard Design, C-F-I Executive Storytelling, Budget & Inventory Analytics.
